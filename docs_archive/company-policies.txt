Lumina Coffee Co. — Customer Policies

REFUNDS AND RETURNS
Customers may return unopened bags of coffee within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund. Opened bags cannot be returned for hygiene reasons, but if you are unhappy with the taste we will replace the bag once, free of charge, under our Freshness Guarantee. Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 5 to 7 business days to appear.

SHIPPING
We ship across India within 3 to 5 business days. Orders above 999 rupees qualify for free standard shipping. Express shipping (1 to 2 business days) is available at checkout for an extra 149 rupees. We currently do not ship internationally.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
Our coffee subscription delivers freshly roasted beans every two or four weeks. You can pause, skip, or cancel any delivery from your account dashboard up to 48 hours before the next dispatch. Subscribers receive a 15 percent discount on every order and free shipping regardless of order value.

SUPPORT HOURS
Our support team is available Monday to Saturday, 9am to 7pm IST. The fastest way to reach us is the live chat on our website. You can also email hello@luminacoffee.example, and we aim to reply within one business day.
