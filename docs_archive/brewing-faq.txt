Lumina Coffee Co. — Brewing FAQ

HOW MUCH COFFEE SHOULD I USE?
A good starting point is a ratio of 1 gram of coffee to 16 grams of water. For a standard mug that is roughly 15 grams of coffee to 240 millilitres of water. Adjust to taste: use more coffee for a stronger cup, less for a milder one.

WHAT WATER TEMPERATURE IS BEST?
Aim for water between 90 and 96 degrees Celsius, just off the boil. Water that is too hot can scorch the coffee and make it taste bitter, while water that is too cool leads to a weak, sour cup. If you do not have a thermometer, let boiled water rest for about 30 seconds before pouring.

WHY DOES MY COFFEE TASTE BITTER?
Bitterness usually means over-extraction. Try a coarser grind, a shorter brew time, or slightly cooler water. Using too much coffee or water that is too hot are the most common causes.

WHY DOES MY COFFEE TASTE SOUR OR WEAK?
Sourness usually means under-extraction. Try a finer grind, a longer brew time, or slightly hotter water. Stale or old beans can also produce a flat, lifeless cup, so check the roast date.

HOW LONG SHOULD BREWING TAKE?
Brew times vary by method. Espresso takes 25 to 30 seconds, an AeroPress about 1 to 2 minutes, a pour-over around 3 minutes, and a French press about 4 minutes. Cold brew is the exception and steeps for 12 to 18 hours in the fridge.

CAN I REHEAT COFFEE?
You can, but it is best fresh. Reheating, especially in a microwave, tends to increase bitterness. If you want hot coffee later, brewing into a pre-warmed insulated flask keeps it fresher than reheating.
