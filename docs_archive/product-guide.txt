Lumina Coffee Co. — Product Guide

OUR ROASTS
We offer three signature roasts. Sunrise is a light roast with bright, fruity notes of citrus and berry, best brewed as a pour-over or in an AeroPress. Midday is a medium roast with a balanced, chocolatey body that works well in almost any brewer, including drip machines. Dusk is a dark roast with bold, smoky notes and low acidity, ideal for espresso and moka pots.

BEAN SOURCING
All Lumina beans are single-origin and ethically sourced. Sunrise beans come from the highlands of Chikmagalur, Karnataka. Midday is a blend sourced from Coorg. Dusk beans are grown in the Baba Budangiri hills. We pay farmers above the fair-trade minimum and publish our sourcing report every year.

GRIND OPTIONS
Every roast is available as whole bean or pre-ground. When ordering pre-ground, choose your grind: coarse for French press and cold brew, medium for drip and pour-over, and fine for espresso. For the freshest cup we recommend buying whole bean and grinding just before brewing.

STORAGE AND FRESHNESS
Store your coffee in an airtight container away from light, heat, and moisture. Do not refrigerate, as this introduces moisture and odors. Beans are best used within four weeks of the roast date, which is printed on the bottom of every bag. Ground coffee is best used within two weeks.

CAFFEINE AND DECAF
Sunrise has the highest caffeine content, followed by Midday, with Dusk the lowest, because darker roasting slightly reduces caffeine. We also offer a Swiss Water decaf version of the Midday roast, which removes 99.9 percent of caffeine without chemicals.
