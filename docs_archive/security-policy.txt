Northwind Analytics — Security and Data Policy

Encryption:
All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and encrypted at rest
using AES-256. Encryption keys are rotated on a regular schedule and managed
through a dedicated key management service.

Compliance:
Northwind Analytics is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. A Data
Processing Addendum (DPA) is available on request. Enterprise customers can
request the latest audit report under NDA.

Access control:
The Enterprise plan supports SSO/SAML and role-based access control with four
roles: Owner, Admin, Analyst, and Viewer. Access to production data is limited
to authorized personnel and every access is logged.

Data residency:
Customers can choose to store data in the United States or the European Union.
Data does not leave the selected region.

Data retention and deletion:
Retention depends on your plan: 30 days on Starter, 12 months on Growth, and
36 months on Enterprise. You can request deletion of a user's data at any time
to support GDPR "right to be forgotten" requests; deletions are completed within
30 days.

Reliability:
The Enterprise plan includes a 99.9% uptime SLA. The platform runs across
multiple availability zones, and encrypted backups are taken daily.

Reporting a vulnerability:
Security issues can be reported to the security team, which acknowledges reports
within one business day and provides a remediation timeline after triage.
