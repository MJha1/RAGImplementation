Northwind Analytics — Product Overview

Northwind Analytics is a cloud platform that helps product and operations teams
turn raw event data into decisions. It ingests data from your app, warehouse,
and third-party tools, then surfaces trends, funnels, and anomalies without
requiring SQL.

Plans and pricing:
- Starter ($0/month): 1 project, up to 3 users, 30-day data retention, core
  dashboards. Best for evaluation and small teams.
- Growth ($199/month): unlimited projects, up to 25 users, 12-month retention,
  funnel and retention analysis, Slack and email alerts.
- Enterprise (custom pricing): unlimited users, 36-month retention, SSO/SAML,
  role-based access control, a dedicated success manager, and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

Key capabilities:
- No-code funnels and retention curves.
- Anomaly detection that flags unexpected drops or spikes in key metrics.
- Cohort analysis to compare behavior across user segments.
- Scheduled reports delivered to Slack or email.
- A REST API and webhooks for exporting insights into other tools.

Integrations include Segment, Snowflake, BigQuery, Amplitude, Salesforce, and
a JavaScript SDK for web and mobile apps. Data typically appears in dashboards
within two minutes of being received.
