Northwind Analytics — Onboarding FAQ

How do I get started?
Create a free Starter account, create your first project, and install the
tracking snippet. You can either paste the JavaScript SDK into your site or send
events through the REST API. Most teams see data flowing within an hour.

How do I send data?
Three options: (1) the JavaScript SDK for web and mobile, (2) the REST API for
server-side events, or (3) a warehouse sync from Snowflake or BigQuery. You can
combine all three in a single project.

How long until I see dashboards?
Data usually appears within two minutes of being received. The first funnel or
retention report is generated automatically once at least 1,000 events arrive.

How do I invite teammates?
Go to Settings > Members and enter their email addresses. Starter supports up to
3 users, Growth up to 25, and Enterprise is unlimited. Invited users receive an
email link and join with the Viewer role by default.

Can I import historical data?
Yes. Use the bulk import endpoint or a warehouse sync to backfill past events.
Backfilled data is subject to your plan's retention window.

What support is available?
Starter and Growth include email support with a one-business-day response time.
Enterprise adds a dedicated success manager and a private Slack channel.

How do I cancel or change plans?
You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from Settings > Billing.
Changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle, and no data is
deleted until the retention window for your new plan lapses.
